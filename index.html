03 / SIMPLE BY DESIGN

From export to insight.

Instagram already lets you download your follower and following data. FollowCheck is designed to make it useful.

Open Instagram Accounts Center ↗
01

Request your data

In Instagram Accounts Center, export Followers and following with the date range set to All time. Choose HTML or JSON.

02

Open the original ZIP

When Instagram prepares the export, download it. The final FollowCheck build will accept the ZIP without manual extraction.

03

Explore your results

Compare relationship lists, review any completeness warnings, and export your findings once real processing is implemented.

PRIVACY IS THE FEATURE

Your Instagram password? Keep it.

A follower comparison should not require surrendering your credentials. Our approach uses your own Instagram exports instead of requesting a username/password or scraping your account.
